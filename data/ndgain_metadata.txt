Dataset: ND-GAIN Country Index
Snapshot year: 2023
Measure used: Overall Country Index score
Scale: 0-100; higher scores are better
Components: Vulnerability and Readiness
Important: This project does not use the separate ND-GAIN Vulnerability or Readiness component.
Snapshot: data/ndgain_2023.csv is a frozen 2023 snapshot used to keep the analysis reproducible when later ND-GAIN releases are published.
Official source: https://gain-new.crc.nd.edu/ranking
Download/source information: https://gain-new.crc.nd.edu/about/download
Methodology: https://gain-new.crc.nd.edu/about/methodology
License/attribution: ND-GAIN states that its data may be downloaded, analyzed, and republished under a Creative Commons license; follow the current official attribution terms.
